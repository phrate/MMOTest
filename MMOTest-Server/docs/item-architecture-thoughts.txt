I'm on the fence about this, because if we merge, there will eventually be a ton of unused columns for a lot of items. I'm going to list a variety of items and these specific items I chose for a reason. They will illustrate the architecture I am looking to implement:

- Air Rune: Non-equippable. Used for casting spells. No special tooltip actions. Probably type "rune".

- Teleport to House: Non-equippable. Used to teleport to house, consumed on use. Special tooltip actions are Break, Inside, Outside, Group. Probably type "tele_tab"

- Seal of Passage: Equippable in the neck slot. Must be in inventory or equipped to converse with any NPCs in the area "Lunar Isle". Special tooltip action is "Break" instead of "Drop" as a lot of quest items have. Probably type "quest".

Then again... I'm now leaning more toward a "tag" system. So Seal of Passage might be tagged "quest", "gear", and "gear_neck", because it's a quest item that you can wear in your neck slot. No stat bonuses. Let's continue anyway with this tag system in mind instead of just one "type".

- Staff of Fire: Equippable in the main-hand slot. Is a weapon that uses runes from the inventory to cast spells. Can be set to autocast a certain spell, otherwise will just whack the enemy as if it were a big stick. Attack bonuses: stab +3, slash -83, crush +9, magic +10. Defense bonuses: stab +2, slash +3, crush +1, magic +10. Possible tags: gear, gear_main_hand, gear_magic, staff, staff_fire, magic.

Lets keep in mind that tags could simply be for categorizing in shops/bank/admin editors/etc. like "magic" or "staff" would be. Or even (for example) for a quest step that needs you to simply equip any type of staff to continue. So it could check if the player has an equipped item with the "staff" tag, making "Staff of Fire" or even an "Ancient Staff" pass the check.

- Mark of Grace: Non-equippable. Doubles as a currency for purchasing "Graceful" gear and as a reagent in Herblore for making "Stamina Potions" (well you trade these in for amylase crystals that are used as an ingredient, but still). You obtain them randomly through training the Agility skill. Possible tags: currency, agility

- Pet cat: Non-equippable. Obtained through a quest. Special case where dropping it actually spawns an npc cat that follows the player, which can then be picked up and put back in the inventory. (This is an extremely interesting item, because there's obviously code just for items that can spawn npc followers.) Possible tags: npc_follower, cat, quest

- Fire element staff crown: Non-equippable. Obtained as a drop from a boss. Useless on its own, but combined with (or Use with/gfs recipe in our case) the "Ice element staff crown" it creates the "Twinflame staff". Possible tags: gear_component, boss_loot

- Helm of Neitiznot: Equippable in the head slot. Obtained from a quest in the Fremmenik questline. Defense bonuses: stab +31, slash +29, crush +34, magic +3, range +30. Extra bonuses: prayer +3, melee str +3. Possible tags: gear, gear_head, fremmenik, fremmenik_gear, quest

- Fire cape: Equippable in the cape slot. Obtained through a boss. Attack bonuses: stab +1, slash +1, crush +1, magic +1, range +1. Defense bonuses: stab +11, slash +11, crush +11, magic +11, range +11. Extra bonuses: prayer +2, melee str +4. Possible tags: gear, gear_cape, boss_loot, tzhaar, tzhaar_gear

- Amulet of strength: Equippable in the neck slot. Obtained through loot, crafting, or trading players/shops. Extra bonuses: melee str +10. Possible tags: gear, gear_neck, jewelry

- Ruby amulet: Equippable in the neck slot. Obtained through loot, crafting, or trading players/shops. No bonuses. Can be enchanted to transform into "Amulet of strength" using the spell "Lvl-3 Enchant" with 1 Cosmic Rune and 5 Fire Runes. Possible tags: gear, gear_neck, jewelry

- Abyssal whip: Equippable in the main-hand slot. Obtained through various monsters. Attack bonuses: slash +82. Extra bonuses: Melee str +82. Possible tags: gear, gear_main_hand, gear_melee, abyssal, abyssal_gear, whip

- Dragon defender: Equippable in the off-hand slot. Obtained through killing Cyclops, but only drops if you have a Rune defender in your inventory. Attack bonuses: stab +25, slash +24, crush +23, magic -3, range -2. Defense bonuses: stab +25, slash +24, crush +23, magic -3, range -3. Possible tags: gear, gear_offhand, gear_offhand_melee, defender, dragon_gear

- Dramen staff: Equippable in the main-hand slot. Obtained through a quest. Must be equipped to use "Fairy Ring Teleports". Has bonuses. (I'll stop listing bonuses from here on unless special cases). Possible tags: gear, gear_main_hand, staff, quest, fairy_ring_gear

- Void ranger helm: Equippable in the head slot. Obtained through a minigame. Has bonuses. Has a special "set effect" when equipped with the rest of the "Void" gear. So if I'm wearing: Void ranger helm, void knight top, void knight robe, and void knight gloves, I get a set effect that gives +10% to ranged damage and accuracy. There are a number of gear sets like this in OSRS with special set effects. Possible tags: gear, gear_head, gear_ranged, void_gear, pest_control_gear, minigame_gear, set_effect_gear

- Slayer helmet (i): Equippable in the head slot. Obtained through crafting. It is the upgraded version of "Slayer helmet". Has bonuses. When worn and fighting the monster you are currently assigned to on your Slayer Task, this helmet gives +15% damage and accuracy to melee, ranged, and magic. Possible tags: gear, gear_head, gear_melee, gear_ranged, gear_magic, slayer_gear, imbued_gear

Next is the the first example of gear that has "charges".

- Warped sceptre: Equppable in the main-hand slot. Obtained through loot. Is a magic sceptre that casts via charges (this is considered a Powered Staff, which are staffs that have charges and cannot autocast from the spellbook.) It must be charged with Earth runes and Chaos runes, holding up to 20,000. Each cast requires 2 chaos runes and 5 earth runes. To fully charge the sceptre, you'd need 40,000 chaos runes and 100,000 earth runes. You can configure the charges by right-clicking the sceptre and clicking Charge. If you have the runes in your inventory, it will ask how many charges you want to add, zero to however many you can with the amount of runes you are holding. You can also right-click Uncharge to take the runes out of the sceptre. Possible tags: gear, gear_main_hand, gear_magic, staff, staff_powered, charged_gear, magic

Next is the first example of gear the "deteriorates"

Torag's helm: Equippable in the head slot. Obtained through looting a boss chest. Has bonuses. Is part of the category of gear known as "Barrows equipment". I'll let you research that page on the osrs wiki for the degradation. It also has a set effect when worn with the other Torag pieces of armor. Possible tags: gear, gear_head, gear_melee, barrows_gear, boss_loot, degrading_gear, set_effect_gear

Ring of dueling: Equippable in the ring slot. Obtained through loot, crafting, or trading players/shops. Special tooltip option is "Rub" which when hovered over, brings up a sub-infobox menu of a list of teleport locations. Holds 8 charges. Rubbing->Teleporting uses up one charge. When the last charge is used, the ring breaks and the item is deleted. Cannot be recharged (unlike some jewelry like the Amulet of Glory that *can* be recharged).

Lobster: Non-equippable. Obtained through loot, cooking a "Raw lobster", or trading players/shops. Heals 12 hitpoints. Possible tags: consumable, consumable_hitpoints, food, food_fishing

Beer: Non-equippable. Obtained through loot, or by "Using" a "Beer glass" on a "Beer barrel" world object. Heals 1 hitpoint and temporarily boosts strength by 2% + 1, while temporarily draining attack by 6% +1. Possible tags: consumable, consumable_hitpoints, consumable_debuff, drink, beer

Irit leaf: Non-equippable. Obtained through loot, or by tooltip action "Clean" on a "Grimy irit leaf". Is an ingredient in multiple products: Aga paste, Antidote++, Irit tar. Possible tags: herb, herb_clean, herblore, ingredient, herblore_ingredient

Next is the first example of an item that has doses instead of charges. It's also the first example of an item or consumable that can boost one of the player's combat levels *above* the base level temporarily.

Saradomin brew: Non-equippable. Obtained through loot, or through Herblore skill. Each dose taken temporarily lowers strength, attack, magic, and ranged by 10% + 2 of their current levels, and temporarily raises hitpoints by 15% + 2 and defense by 20% + 2 of their base levels, all rounded down. This can boost the player's hitpoints and defense above their base level by up to the amount restored. They're frequrently used alongside "Super restore" potions which restore the drained offensive stats and can leave the player with overhealed hitpoints and a temporarily boosted defense level greater than they would gain from a "Super defense potion". Possible tags: consumable, potion, herblore, boosting_consumable, overhealing_consumable

Bucket of water: Non-equippable. Obtained through loot, item combination, or trading players/shops. Is used in various quests. Is an ingredient for various things like ales, doughs,  pies, clay. Possible tags: bucket, water_source, water_source_small, ingredient

Bag of salt: Non-equippable. Obtained through purchase from a Slayer Master. Is used to finish off rockslugs (killable npc) that have less than 5 HP. Rockslugs can only be killed/taken to 0 HP by using this item on them. Possible tags: slayer_tool, salt_source, salt_source_small
